# Mushroom Aglio Olio (Porch Calgary–Inspired)

Approximation of the Mushroom Aglio Olio from Porch in Calgary  
Featuring mixed mushrooms, garlic-forward olive oil, parmesan, and pappardelle.

---

# Version 1 — Restaurant-Style (Weekend / Full Flavor)

## Ingredients (2–3 servings)

### Pasta & sauce
- 250–300 g pappardelle (fresh preferred)
- 5–6 tbsp good olive oil
- 6–8 cloves garlic
- ½ tsp chili flakes (optional)
- ¼–½ cup grated parmesan, plus more to finish
- Splash pasta water
- Salt + black pepper
- Lemon zest (optional)

### Mushrooms
- ~400 g mixed mushrooms:
  - Pink oyster
  - Blue oyster
  - Lion’s mane
  - Pioppino
  - Sub: shiitake, cremini, king oyster
- Salt + pepper
- Small knob butter (optional)

---

## Step 1 — Confit garlic (key flavor)
1. Slice garlic.
2. Place in small pan with olive oil to just cover.
3. Cook on very low heat 10–15 min until soft and lightly golden.
4. Reserve garlic and oil.

---

## Step 2 — Cook mushrooms
1. Heat wide pan until hot.
2. Add olive oil.
3. Add mushrooms in a single layer.
4. Do not stir for 2–3 min → let brown.
5. Season salt + pepper.
6. Finish with knob of butter.

Remove and set aside.

---

## Step 3 — Build aglio olio base
1. Cook pappardelle in salted water.
2. In pan:
   - Add 3–4 tbsp garlic confit oil
   - Add some confit garlic
   - Add chili flakes (optional)
3. Warm gently.

---

## Step 4 — Emulsify & finish
1. Add cooked pasta to pan.
2. Add mushrooms.
3. Add splash pasta water + parmesan.
4. Toss vigorously until glossy and lightly creamy.
5. Finish with:
   - Black pepper
   - Lemon zest (optional)
   - More parmesan

---

## Restaurant-style upgrades
- Splash white wine before pasta
- Add mushroom stock
- Finish with parsley or chives
- Top with toasted breadcrumbs

# Version 2 — Weeknight 20-Minute Version

## Ingredients (2 servings)

- 250 g pappardelle (or any wide pasta)
- 300–400 g mushrooms (any mix)
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- ¼–⅓ cup grated parmesan
- Chili flakes (optional)
- Salt + black pepper
- Splash pasta water
- Optional: squeeze lemon or pinch lemon zest

---

## Method

### 1) Start pasta
- Boil salted water and cook pasta.

### 2) Brown mushrooms
- Heat pan hot → add olive oil.
- Add mushrooms.
- Leave untouched 2–3 min so they brown.
- Salt + pepper.

### 3) Garlic oil
- Push mushrooms aside.
- Add garlic + chili flakes.
- Cook gently 30–60 sec until fragrant (not browned).

### 4) Toss
- Add drained pasta directly to pan.
- Add splash pasta water + parmesan.
- Toss 30–60 sec until glossy.

### 5) Finish
- Black pepper
- More parmesan
- Optional squeeze lemon

Serve immediately.

---

## Weeknight shortcuts
- Skip confit garlic
- Use one mushroom type
- Pre-grated parm is fine
- No herbs required

---

## Quick “restaurant boost”
Right before serving:
- Drizzle 1 tsp good olive oil
- Add extra parmesan
- Fresh cracked pepper

Makes it taste like a restaurant version instantly.
